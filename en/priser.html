The time bank

What is the time bank?

The time bank is your family's account for tutoring hours. You fill it with a plan, which makes each hour cheaper and is valid for 30 days. When you propose a session for one child the hours pay for it by themselves. If a session paid with hours ends before a full hour, the rest is saved in the time bank, and the minutes cover overtime on later sessions or pay for a whole session when there are enough of them.

If you pay session by session, the time bank is not used: each session is paid on its own, as usual. Saved minutes never expire. Siblings share the time bank, and you find it under Profile in your view.

The hours are drawn when you propose a session, for one child per session, and if the tutor declines they come back. If you stop before they are used, you get back what is left, with the hours used counted at the regular price. If you withdraw from the purchase within 14 days, the hours used are counted at the price you paid instead. Terms for the offers

Add-ons

More children in the same session.

If you have two children who need help at the same time, you do not need to book two sessions. The tutor takes both during the same hour — but their attention is divided, and that should show in the price.

How payment works

Everything goes through Nextrum.

Booking, payment and completed session all connect in one place. You see what was booked, what was paid and what took place.

  1. 01

    You book a session

    You suggest a time in your view and the tutor confirms it. If you only need help before a test, you book that session and nothing more.

  2. 02

    Pay now

    You pay for each session by card or Klarna as soon as the tutor has confirmed the time, and if the session is shorter than booked we refund the difference. If you have bought hours in a plan or in the time bank, they pay for the session by themselves.

  3. 03

    The session takes place

    You pay for the time the session actually lasted. Afterwards the tutor writes a report on what you covered and how it went.

  4. 04

    Read the report

    You read the report in your view. If the session was shorter than booked, we refund the difference ourselves.
