Add-ons

More children in the same session.

If you have two children who need help at the same time, you do not need to book two sessions. The tutor takes both during the same hour — but their attention is divided, and that should show in the price.

Extra child
69 kr / child and hour

Added to the hourly price for each child beyond the first.

What it comes to
  • One child, one hour379 kr
  • Two children, one hour448 kr
  • Three children, one hour517 kr

The same applies to longer sessions — the add-on is counted per hour, just like the hourly price. The children do not have to be in the same year group, but the session works best when they are working on related subjects.

Tell us in the enquiry how many children it concerns, so we can allow for it already in the matching.

How payment works

Everything goes through Nextrum.

No cash, no transfers between student and tutor. That is safer for both, and makes it easier to keep track of what actually took place.

  • 01
    You pay per completed session

    Nothing is charged before a session has taken place. If you only need help before a test, you book that session and nothing more.

  • 02
    The same price whatever the subject

    Maths, Swedish, physics or programming all cost the same. You do not have to work out a new price every time the need changes.

  • 03
    No lock-in

    You can pause between terms, change tutor or stop altogether, with no notice period.

  • 04
    The report is included

    After each session you get a report on what was covered and how it went, plus access to extra digital exercises.

Why the price looks like this

Young people helping young people — you can see it in the price.

Traditional private tuition is built on qualified teachers on a teacher’s salary. Nextrum is built on upper-secondary and university students who recently took the same course.

That makes the help cheaper — but above all it often makes it easier to follow. A tutor who sat with the same chapter two years ago remembers exactly where it tends to go wrong, and can explain it in words the student recognises.

At the same time it means the money goes to someone getting their first job and their first work experience. That is the whole point of Nextrum.
