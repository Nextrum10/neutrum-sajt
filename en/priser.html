How payment works

Everything goes through Nextrum.

Booking, payment and completed session all connect in one place. You see what was booked, what was paid and what took place.

  1. 01

    You book a session

    You suggest a time in your view and the tutor confirms it. If you only need help before a test, you book that session and nothing more.

  2. 02

    Pay in advance if you like

    You pay for each session by card, either in advance or after the session when you confirm the report. In advance works as soon as the time is confirmed, and if you have bought hours in a plan or as prepaid hours, they pay for the session by themselves.

  3. 03

    The session takes place

    You pay for the time the session actually lasted. Afterwards the tutor writes a report on what you covered and how it went.

  4. 04

    Confirm and pay

    You read the report in your view and confirm it, and pay by card if the session is not already paid. After the session you can choose an invoice instead, which comes at the start of the following month with ten days to pay and no fee.

Why the price looks like this

Young people helping young people —
you can see it in the price.

Traditional private tuition is built on qualified teachers on a teacher’s salary. Nextrum is built on tutors who recently took the same course.

That makes the help cheaper — but above all it often makes it easier to follow. A tutor who sat with the same chapter two years ago remembers exactly where it tends to go wrong, and can explain it in words the student recognises.

At the same time it means the money goes to someone getting their first job and their first work experience. That is the whole point of Nextrum.
