Cookies and storage

A short page, because there is little to report: we set no cookies at all.

We set no cookies

That is why there is no banner here asking for your consent. It would be asking permission for nothing.

We measured this rather than assumed it: the public pages set zero cookies, and nothing is stored in your browser until you log in.

We do count visits. How that works without cookies is explained just below.

What is stored when you log in

When you log in, your session is saved in your own browser, in what is called localStorage. That is why you do not have to log in again on every page.

  • It never leaves your device except to our login provider.
  • It requires no consent: it is necessary for something you asked for yourself.
  • It disappears when you log out. You can also clear it yourself through your browser’s site data settings.

How we count visits

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see how many people find us and which pages they read. It works without cookies, which is the entire reason we chose it.

Instead of marking your browser, unique visitors are derived from a hash of your IP address and your browser type, with a salt that changes every day. The raw IP address is not stored, and the hash cannot be reversed. After a day the salt changes and the same visitor gets a different value — so nothing follows you over time.

What is collected is which page was read, where you came from, country, operating system, browser, and whether you were on a computer or a phone. None of it identifies you.

Measurement exists only on the public pages. The student view and the tutor view are not measured at all.

What is not here

  • No cookie — not even for the statistics above
  • No advertising trackers, no pixel from any social network
  • No third-party cookies whatsoever
  • No profiling and no automated decisions about you
  • Nothing that follows you between sites, or between days

The third parties that are still loaded

Even without cookies, every visit means your browser fetches files from someone. This is the entire list:

  • Vercel — the site is hosted there, so all traffic passes through them. They are also the ones counting visits, in the way described above.
  • Supabase — the database and the login. Contacted when you submit a form or log in. The servers are within the EU.

That is the whole list — and it has grown shorter. The fonts used to sit with Google, and the library the login needs used to sit with unpkg.com. Both sent your IP address to a third party on every page view. Both now sit with us, and those transfers no longer happen.

If that changes

If we ever add something that genuinely requires your consent — embedded video, advertising tools, anything that follows you between sites — we will ask first, and this page will be updated at the same time. We will not put a consent banner on the site in advance for the sake of appearances, and we will not add measurement quietly: the visit counting above was added after this page was first published, and the page was rewritten the same day.
