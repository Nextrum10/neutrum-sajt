Privacy policy

What we store, why, who can see it and for how long. Written to be read, not to hide behind.

Who is responsible

The limited company Nextrum AB is being formed and is not yet registered. Until then we are the data controllers as private individuals — not a company. We say so plainly because it is true, and so that you know who you are actually giving your details to. Once the company is registered this page will be updated with its registration number.

In short

We store what is needed to match a student with the right tutor, to book and run the sessions, to keep you informed about what is happening, and to invoice for what took place. Nothing beyond that.

We do not sell data. We do not pass it on for marketing. We set no cookies.

We do count how many people visit the public pages, but without cookies and without anything that follows you over time. How that works is explained under Cookies and storage.

What we store

About you as a parent or student with an account

  • Name, email address and — if you fill it in — phone number
  • A profile picture, if you upload one
  • We never see your password. It is handled encrypted by our login provider and is inaccessible even to us.

About the child

  • Name, year group, school and the subjects concerned
  • Study goals, if you fill them in
  • Homework, skill areas and material the tutor adds
  • Reports after each session: what you did, how it went, what needs practice

Your own notes about the child are private even from us. They sit in a separate table where neither tutor nor administrator has read access — that is a technical barrier in the database, not a promise to refrain from looking. We moved them there for exactly that reason: as long as they sat in the same row as the rest of the student record, a tutor could have read them through the API even though no screen displayed them.

About you if you apply to be a tutor

  • Name, age, email, school, subjects and when you can work
  • What you write in the free-text field, and your CV if you attach one
  • If you are accepted: your profile text, town, subjects and the hourly rate we agreed

About the sessions and the contact

  • Date, time, subject, length and format for each booked session
  • Messages between family and tutor in the platform’s chat
  • Invoices and payment statements: periods, hours and amounts

Who can see what

Access is restricted in the database, row by row — not only in the interface. That means the restriction holds even for someone who tried to go around the site.

  • A tutor sees only the students they are matched with, and only their own sessions, homework, material and messages.
  • A family sees only their own children, sessions, invoices and messages.
  • We can reach what is needed to run the service and solve problems — with the exception of your private notes, described above.
  • Files — material and profile pictures — sit in closed storage. Each file opens through a time-limited link that expires after an hour. If such a link is passed on, it does not work for long.

Where the data lives

  • Supabase — database, login and files. The servers are within the EU (Ireland).
  • Vercel — host of the site itself, and the party counting visits on the public pages. The counting happens without cookies: unique visitors are derived from a hash of IP and browser type with a salt that changes daily, and the raw IP address is not stored. The student view and the tutor view are not measured.

Beyond those, nothing is fetched from anyone else. The fonts used to sit with Google and the library the login needs used to sit with unpkg.com — both sent your IP address there on every page view. Both now sit with us.

How long we keep things

  • Account and study history — for as long as you are customers, and after that until you ask us to delete them.
  • Invoice records — seven years. That is required by Swedish accounting law and is not something we can shorten, not even on request.
  • Applications that do not lead to work — at most one year, so that we can get in touch if something comes up. If you want it deleted sooner, write to us.
  • Enquiries — until we have replied and the matter is closed.

Children’s data

The service is about children, and their data is therefore the most sensitive thing we handle.

  • Children do not have their own accounts. It is the parent who registers, accepts the terms and enters the details about the child.
  • We collect only what the teaching requires — year group, school, subjects and goals. No personal identity numbers, no diagnoses, no grades.
  • As a parent you can see, change and delete what is recorded about your child at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to know what we have stored about you, to have it corrected if it is wrong, to have it erased, to restrict how we use it, to object to the processing, and to receive your data in a format you can take with you. Write to info@nextrum.se.

If you think we are handling your data wrongly, you have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). Do get in touch with us first — it is usually quicker to sort out.

Cookies and browser storage

We set no cookies — not even for the visit counting. What is actually stored, what is measured and why, is on its own page: Cookies and storage.

Changes

If we change anything material we update the date below, and contact those of you with an account if the change affects you.
