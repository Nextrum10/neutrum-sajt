Swedish school,
English at home?

When the homework is in Swedish and you are still learning the language, it is hard to help. We match your child with a young tutor who recently took the same courses in a Swedish school, at home in Stockholm or online.

Get started
How it works

From the form
to the first session.

The same service as for every other family, with one difference: you tell us which language the sessions should be in.

  • 01
    Tell us what you need

    Fill in the interest form in English: the subject, the year group, where in Stockholm you live, and which language the sessions should be in. It costs nothing, and you are not committing to anything.

  • 02
    We match, and we are honest about the language

    We look for a tutor who recently took the same courses. If you want the sessions in English, we tell you before anything is booked whether we have someone who can take them in English.

  • 03
    At home or online

    You choose for each session. At home works when the tutor can get to you every week; otherwise you start online.

  • 04
    A report after every session

    The tutor writes what you went through and what comes next. The family view, where you book and read the reports, is in Swedish for now.

What we help with

The homework,
and the Swedish.

Swedish and Swedish as a second language, maths, English and the science subjects, from year 1 to upper secondary school. For a child who is new to Swedish school, the first step is often to understand what the homework is asking, before the subject itself.

We match on the Swedish curriculum, so the tutor knows what your child is expected to learn this year and how the school will assess it.

Price

SEK 399
an hour.

The same price in every subject, at home or online. Siblings in the same session add SEK 69 an hour in total, the same for three children as for two. No registration fee and no minimum term, and for new families the first hour is free once you have booked two hours.
