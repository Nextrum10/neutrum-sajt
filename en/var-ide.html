To the home page Our idea

The right person can make
a real difference.

Nextrum started from a simple observation: someone who recently struggled with a course themselves often explains it better than someone who knows it by heart.

The background

Whoever just understood it
explains it best.

A student who is stuck rarely needs more facts. They need someone who remembers what it felt like not to understand — and who can say where it usually goes wrong, because it went wrong for them last year.

That is why our tutors are young. They are at upper-secondary school or university and sat the same courses recently. They remember which step in the working is the hard one, not just what the answer is.

This is not a way to keep the price down. It is the reason we exist.

Our tutors

Selected, interviewed
and prepared.

We put the time into the selection so you do not have to. It is one of the things you are paying us for.

First Application and interview

Subjects, year groups and hours — and a conversation where we get to know the person behind the application.

Then Approval and preparation

Only after approval are you visible to a family. We go through how a session is structured and how the report is written.

Ongoing Follow-up

We follow up on sessions and collect feedback. The work does not stop at the match.

Price

One hourly price. No hidden fees.

The same price whatever the subject. Maths costs the same as Swedish, and a hard course does not cost more than an easy one.

No registration fee, no monthly fee and no minimum term. You pay in arrears for the sessions that actually took place — a session that never happened is never invoiced.

All payment goes through Nextrum. That is the basis for you being able to see what was booked, what took place and what was invoiced — gathered in one place.
