About Nextrum

How it works

You get in touch, we go through the need together with you, and then we select the tutor who suits the student best — based on subject, level, need and personality. So you do not browse a catalogue: we do the matching for you, because the wrong match is worse than no match.

Because a tutor who is brilliant at Year 9 maths is not automatically the right person for a Year 4 child who has lost their confidence. We go through the need with you first and then choose someone. It costs us an extra day and saves you weeks.

The most common are maths, Swedish and English, but we also arrange help in the sciences, humanities, modern languages and programming. Tell us what you need when you get in touch and we will say whether we have the right person.

Yes. You choose whether sessions are held online or in person, and can switch between the two from session to session. Online works for most subjects and makes it easier to find the right person wherever you live.

The tutor writes a report on what was covered and how it went, and you get access to extra digital exercises. The reports collect in the student view so you can see progress over time.

It is the logged-in view where student and parent see the study plan, upcoming sessions, reports and progress in one place. It unlocks once the matching is complete.

Sessions are booked in whole hours — one, two or three — within the times the tutor has entered. You choose the length when you book, and it can differ from one session to the next.

Yes. Both you and the tutor can move or cancel a session until it has taken place, and the other party sees the change immediately in their view. A session that never took place is never invoiced.

If the student does not turn up without cancelling, the session counts as completed and is invoiced. The tutor kept the time free. If you need to cancel, doing it in your view is enough, and it works right up to the start of the session.

Only once the tutor has written the report. That is the same thing that decides what ends up on the invoice — no report, no invoice line.

Price and payment

What it costs

Tutoring costs SEK 379 an hour. The price includes matching, the study plan, booking, administration and payment handling. There is no registration fee and no minimum term. See the full pricing page.

Payment goes through Nextrum. You never pay the tutor directly, and no money changes hands during the session. That is safer for both parties and makes it easier to keep track of what actually took place.

That is up to you. Some families book a session a week, others only before a test. There is no minimum you have to reach.

Yes. There is no minimum term and no notice period. You can pause, change tutor or stop altogether — just get in touch.

No. Getting in touch and talking to us about what you need is free, and you are not tied to anything.

Once a month. The sessions that actually took place during the month are collected on one invoice, and payment terms are 20 days. You always pay in arrears, never up front.

We may pause new bookings until the invoice is settled. Sessions already booked are not affected automatically — get in touch if something goes wrong, it can almost always be sorted.

No. All payment goes through Nextrum, never directly between a family and a tutor. That is the basis for you being able to see what was booked, what took place and what was invoiced — and for the tutor being paid correctly for the right hours.

If you want to work with us

Being a tutor

Anyone at upper-secondary school or university who is confident in the subjects they want to help with. We look just as much at how you explain things and treat others as at your grades. Read more and apply.

Everyone who applies goes through the application, an interview and Nextrum’s checks before being approved. Only then can they be matched with a student. After each session we collect feedback, and anyone who does not measure up gets no further assignments.

You decide which subjects you take, how much you work and when. There is no minimum — but the student does best with the same person week after week, so expect to keep to what you say yes to.

All payment goes through Nextrum, never directly between student and tutor. You can see your hours worked in the tutor view.

You apply, we interview, and you can start only once you have been approved. Until then you are not visible to any family.

Yes. You enter your own times, choose your subjects and decide how much you work. Families can only book within the times you have entered — if you enter none, you cannot be booked at all.

A session that never took place earns nothing and is not invoiced to the family either. If the student does not turn up without cancelling, the session counts as completed, because you kept the time free.

Safety

Safety and privacy

Every tutor goes through Nextrum’s selection and vetting process before being matched with a student. Payment goes through Nextrum and communication through the platform, so the parent always has oversight of what is booked and said.

We never sell your data on. We use it to match you, to contact you and to handle booking and payment.

Get in touch and we will change it. It is no trouble and costs nothing — a match that does not work is worse for everyone.

We are responsible for running the service — matching, the platform, payment, and for the tutors being checked. We do not promise study results. It would not be honest to, and nobody who promises it can keep it.

Yes. There is no minimum term, no notice period and no monthly fee. Invoices for sessions already completed remain, but nothing beyond that.
