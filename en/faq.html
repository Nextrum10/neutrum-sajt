About Nextrum

How it works

You get in touch, we go through the need together with you, and then we select the tutor who suits the student best — based on subject, level, need and personality. So you do not browse a catalogue: we do the matching for you, because the wrong match is worse than no match.

Because a tutor who is brilliant at Year 9 maths is not automatically the right person for a Year 4 child who has lost their confidence. We go through the need with you first and then choose someone. It costs us an extra day and saves you weeks.

No, and we do not call them teachers. A tutor at Nextrum has recently taken the same courses as the student, but is not a trained teacher. That is also the point: they remember where they got stuck and explain in a way that is close to the student. If you are looking for a certified teacher, we are the wrong choice, and we would rather say so now than after the first session.

Maths, Swedish, English and the sciences, and we also arrange help in the humanities and modern languages. Tell us what you need when you get in touch and we will say whether we have the right person.

Yes. You choose whether sessions are held online or in person, and can switch between the two from session to session. Online works for most subjects and makes it easier to find the right person wherever you live.

The tutor writes a report on what was covered and how it went, and you get access to extra digital exercises. The reports collect in the student view so you can see progress over time.

It is the logged-in view where student and parent see the study plan, upcoming sessions, reports and progress in one place. You can log in as soon as we have taken you on, and booking opens once the matching is complete.

Sessions are whole hours — one, two or three. You suggest the day, time and length, and the tutor accepts it or suggests another. The length can differ from one session to the next.

Yes, until 12 hours before the session starts. Both you and the tutor can move a session, and the other party sees the change immediately in their view. A session that is not paid yet you cancel yourselves in your view. If it is already paid, get in touch and we will cancel it and refund you. If you cancel less than 12 hours before, it is a late cancellation: you pay half the session, or half the time is taken from your hours, and in the last 12 hours the session cannot be moved. It is set out in the terms.

If the student does not turn up without cancelling, the session counts as completed and must be paid. If it has already been paid, the amount is not refunded. The tutor kept the time free. If you need to cancel, do it at least 12 hours before: in your view if the session is not paid yet, otherwise by getting in touch with us. If you cancel later, you pay half the session.

Price and payment

What it costs

Tutoring costs SEK 399 an hour with no commitment, and that is the price, not a starting price. With a plan, one month at a time, you pay less for every hour. The price includes matching, the study plan, booking, administration and payment handling, and there is no registration fee. See the full pricing page.

Yes, the first hour is free when you buy your first plan: the plan gets one extra hour. It is used first and is valid for 60 days from the purchase, even if the plan's other hours have expired. It applies once per family.

Yes, and it costs nothing on top of the sessions. If the student does their part during the school year and the grade in a subject still doesn’t go up, you get extra homework help free of charge. You sign up for the guarantee in the study view, by 31 December for the school year, and help with other subjects does not affect it. The terms of the grade guarantee.

You pay for each session by card or Klarna as soon as the tutor has confirmed the time, and if the session is shorter than booked we refund the difference. After the session the tutor writes a report, which you read in the student view. Booking, payment and session all connect in the student view, so you can always see what you have paid for.

That is up to you. You can book a session a week or only before a test. There is no minimum you have to reach.

No. Getting in touch and talking to us about what you need is free, and you are not tied to anything.

You pay with Pay now as soon as the tutor has confirmed the time, by card or Klarna. If you do not manage to pay before the session it still takes place, and you then pay afterwards under Payment: a session that has taken place must be paid, and if it is left unpaid we will get in touch. If the payment gives you trouble, get in touch — it can almost always be sorted.

If you want to work with us

Being a tutor

Anyone who has started upper secondary school, is allowed to work and is confident in the subjects they want to help with. After upper secondary you do not need to be studying. We look just as much at how you explain things and treat others as at your grades. Read more and apply.

Everyone who applies goes through the application, an interview and Nextrum’s checks before being approved. Only then can they be matched with a student. After each session we collect feedback, and anyone who does not measure up gets no further assignments.

You decide which subjects you take, how much you work and when. There is no required number of hours, but it counts in your favour if you can work at least 4 hours a week. The student does best with the same person week after week, so expect to keep to what you say yes to.

Payment is made for sessions that were completed and reported. You see your hours and what you have earned in the tutor view, and Nextrum handles the payout.

You apply, we interview, and you can start only once you have been approved. Until then you are not visible to any family.

Yes. The family suggests a time, and you accept it or suggest another. No session happens without your yes. You also choose your subjects and decide how much you work.

A session that never took place earns nothing. If the student does not turn up without cancelling, the session counts as completed, because you kept the time free.

Safety

Safety and privacy

Every tutor has been interviewed, has taken our training and passed a test before being matched with a student. Payment goes through Nextrum and communication through the platform, so the parent always has oversight of what is booked and said.

We never sell your data on. We use it to match you, to contact you and to handle booking and payment.

Get in touch and we will change it. It is no trouble and costs nothing — a match that does not work is worse for everyone.

We are responsible for running the service — matching, the platform, payment, and for the tutors being interviewed and trained. We do not promise study results. It would not be honest to, and nobody who promises it can keep it.

Yes. There is no minimum term, no notice period and no monthly fee, whether you pay session by session or with a plan. You can pause, change tutor or stop altogether. You only pay for the sessions you book, and paid sessions that never took place are refunded. A plan runs one month at a time: if you stop before the hours are used, you get back what is left, with the hours you used counted at the regular price.
