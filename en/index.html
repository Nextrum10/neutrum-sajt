Our idea

The right person can make
all the difference.

At Nextrum we match every student with one of our tutors, based on what the student needs and the subjects where support is wanted. Together they build better understanding, more motivation and greater confidence at school.

You get a personal study plan, can book sessions online or in person, and receive a structured report after every one. That way you can easily follow the student’s progress between sessions. We take care of matching, booking and planning — with no registration fee and no minimum term.

Safe help, personal support

Safe help.
Personal support.

When young people help young people it has to be safe for everyone. That is why we build safety into every step, rather than checking afterwards.

  • The same person every time. They remember what was hard last week, because they were there — so no session starts over with a fresh assessment.

  • We go through the need with you first, then pick someone based on subject, level and personality. If it does not work, say so and we rematch. There is no lock-in that makes it awkward.

  • The format is chosen per session, not in a contract. One session at home this week and one online the next needs no change anywhere. Sessions run one to three hours, at times you and the tutor agree on.

  • Booking, messages and payment happen in the platform. Communication goes through us, and booking, payment and session are linked — so it is always possible to see what was actually delivered.

  • The tutor writes a report after every session describing what you covered and how it went. A session only counts as completed once it is written. Student and parent see the same thing.

  • Every tutor goes through our selection and vetting process. All payment goes through Nextrum — never directly between family and tutor. We follow up on sessions and collect feedback.

We are building the next generation together.
