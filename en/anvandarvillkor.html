Terms of use

What you can expect from us, and what we expect from you. No surprises in the small print — there is no small print.

What Nextrum is

Nextrum arranges tutoring. We match a student with a tutor, provide the platform where sessions are booked and followed up, and handle payment and the tutors’ pay.

We are not a school and we do not promise a particular grade or result. What we do promise is that the tutor was selected by us, that you can see what happens between sessions, and that you can switch or stop whenever you like. With the grade guarantee we also promise what we do if the grade does not go up.

Accounts

  • The account is created by an adult. Children do not register themselves.
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of the details you provide, and for keeping your password to yourself.
  • An account is personal and must not be shared.

Booking, moving and cancelling

  • Sessions are whole hours, one to three. The family suggests a time, and the tutor accepts it or suggests another. A session is booked only once both have said yes.
  • A session can be moved or cancelled by either party until it has taken place, subject to the next point. Whoever cancels gives a reason, and the other party gets an email about the cancellation with the reason. A session that has already been paid is cancelled by contacting us.
  • Cancel at least 12 hours before. You cancel a booked session at least 12 hours before it starts. If you cancel it later, it is a late cancellation, and the session counts as held for half the booked time. If you pay for the session with money, you pay half the price, with Pay now as for a session, and if it has already been paid we refund the rest. If the session was paid with hours, half the time is taken from them, as when a session is shorter than booked (see Plans and the time bank): what is left of a bought hour is saved in your time bank, but a started free hour, and an hour free of charge for a recommendation, is used. The same applies when we cancel the session because you asked us to less than 12 hours before. In the last 12 hours you cannot move a booked session either: write to your tutor, who can move it. A proposal that nobody has said yes to yet can always be withdrawn free of charge, and if the tutor or we cancel the session it costs nothing. The rule applies to sessions proposed after you accepted this version of the terms, and it does not limit your right of withdrawal.
  • A session counts as completed only once the tutor has written the report. A session that never took place costs nothing, except for a late cancellation: if it has already been paid, we refund the full amount, or whatever exceeds the fee.
  • If the student does not turn up without cancelling, the session counts as completed and must be paid. If it has already been paid, the amount is not refunded.

Price and payment

  • Nextrum is your counterparty for the payment. You pay us, you contact us if an amount looks wrong, and we are the ones who refund. The tutor is never your counterparty in a question about money.
  • The hourly price is shown on the pricing page, and the price that applies is the one shown at the time of booking.
  • Family discount. We may give your family a discount of a number of kronor per hour, for example through a partner. It is taken off every hour you pay for with money: on sessions, plans and hours for a plan. The price for more children and hours that are already free are not affected. The price with the discount is shown in your view when you book or buy and at checkout, before you pay, and a discount code is calculated on that price. The discount applies until further notice, to what you book and buy while you have it. We may change or remove it, and what you have already booked or bought keeps its price. How it is counted if you stop is set out under Plans and the time bank.
  • You pay for each session by card or Klarna as soon as the tutor has confirmed the time, and if the session is shorter than booked we refund the difference. You pay with Pay now in your view, on the session or under Payment. If you have several sessions to pay, you can pay them all at once under Payment, in one checkout with a line per session, or session by session. Each session is then paid as if it had been paid on its own. If you do not manage to pay before the session, it still takes place, and a session that has taken place must be paid: it then stays under Payment, and we remind you. After the session the tutor writes a report, which you read in your view.
  • The price applies to the time the session actually lasted, counted per started quarter of an hour. The tutor enters the time in the report, and if the session ran longer or shorter than booked, it says so there, with the reason. If the time is wrong, write to us. If you have already paid and the session ran longer, you pay the extra with Pay now under Payment. If it was shorter, we refund the difference ourselves, to the card or Klarna, once the report is written. If the session was paid with hours from a plan or the time bank, hours that were not started come back automatically, and what was left of the last hour is saved in your time bank.
  • If you have hours in your time bank, for example from a plan, your hours pay for the session instead, automatically, as soon as you propose it. See Plans and the time bank below.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. You give your card details directly to Stripe, never to us. If you pay with Klarna you do so with Klarna, on Klarna's terms for the payment option you choose there, and Klarna pays us. We do not save any cards.
  • No registration fee, no monthly fee and no minimum term. You pay for the sessions you book, for half of a session you cancel less than 12 hours before (see Cancel at least 12 hours before) and for hours you have chosen to buy in advance, nothing else.

The sibling supplement

If more children take part in a session, each hour costs more. The price for more children is on the pricing page, and it is the same with one or two siblings. This applies when you book siblings for a session and have accepted the terms for the sibling supplement in the booking: the supplement for the siblings is then paid separately, after the session.

  • What the supplement is. The price for more children times the booked length of the session. The amount is set when the session is booked and does not change afterwards. The session itself costs as for one child and is paid like other sessions.
  • When it is due. Once the session has taken place, the supplement becomes a debt on your account. If you cancel the session less than 12 hours before it starts, half of the supplement becomes a debt, like half of the session (see Cancel at least 12 hours before). If the session is cancelled in time, or by the tutor or us, there is no supplement to pay.
  • How you pay it. With Pay supplement now under Payment in your view, whenever you like, by card or Klarna. If it is still unpaid when you buy hours (a plan or hours for a plan), it is added as a separate line in that purchase. It is never paid with hours, and a discount code or a family discount does not apply to it.
  • Reminder and block. If a supplement has been unpaid for 14 days, we remind you. If the unpaid amount is over 1,000 kr, or a supplement has been unpaid for more than 45 days, you cannot book siblings for a session until it is paid. Sessions for one child are booked as usual.
  • The children and the time are fixed. Which children take part and how long the session is cannot be changed after booking. If more or fewer children should take part, cancel and book a new session.
  • Errors and refunds. If you have paid a supplement that should not have been paid, we refund it, to the card or Klarna. The purchase terms below and your right of withdrawal apply as for the session.

Purchase terms

These apply when you pay us, for a session or several sessions at once, an extra charge for a session that ran longer, the sibling supplement, a late cancellation, a plan, a custom package or hours for a plan. Before checkout opens, you tick a box to confirm that you have read them.

  • Who you buy from. You buy from us, as set out under Who the agreement is with at the top of this page, and you contact us about the purchase: info@nextrum.se.
  • What you buy. The box before checkout says what the purchase is for. How sessions are booked and paid for is set out above, and what the hours give you, how long they are valid and how they are used is set out under Plans and the time bank.
  • The price. The price in kronor is shown in checkout before you pay, with any discount you have taken off, and we add no fees.
  • How you pay. By card or Klarna, in Stripe's checkout. Apple Pay and Google Pay also work when checkout shows them, since they pay with a card. If you choose Klarna, Klarna's terms for the payment option you choose there apply, and Klarna decides whether it is possible. We do not take other payment methods.
  • When the purchase is complete. A purchase of hours is complete when the payment has been approved. The hours are then in your time bank, and a plan is valid for 30 days from that day. Stripe emails a receipt to the address on your account.
  • If the payment does not go through. If the card is declined or Klarna says no, nothing has been paid, and you can try again with Pay now, with another card or payment method. The session is not cancelled because the payment did not go through, and it must be paid once it has taken place.
  • Right of withdrawal. You have a 14-day right of withdrawal, for a session from the day it was booked and for a purchase of hours from the day of purchase. How you withdraw and what we then refund is set out under Right of withdrawal.
  • Refunds. Money we refund goes to the card you paid with, or to Klarna, which deducts it from what you owe them. If we refund a purchase of hours, the amount is calculated as under Stopping in Plans and the time bank, or under Right of withdrawal during the first 14 days, and the hours we refund are removed from your time bank. An hour that has already paid for a session counts as used.
  • A payment that is wrong. If an amount looks wrong, if you have paid for the same thing twice or if you did not get what you paid for, write to us. We refund what should not have been taken. If we cannot agree, what is set out under Disputes applies.

Plans and the time bank

You fill your time bank with hours in advance with a plan, which is valid for 30 days and has a lower hourly price, or with a custom package we have sent you (see Custom package). If you have a plan you can also buy more hours for it (see Buy more hours). Fill the time bank, hours at the regular hourly price, is no longer sold, but hours you have already bought with it are valid as before, and what is set out here applies to them too. If you pay session by session the time bank is not used, and each session is paid as under Price and payment above. Which plans are available and what they cost is shown on the pricing page, and a custom package is shown in your view. It is a one-off purchase, by card or Klarna. It is not a subscription and does not renew by itself.

  • How long the hours are valid. A plan is valid for 30 days from the day it is paid, and is valid straight away. Hours bought with Fill the time bank are valid for 6 months from the purchase, however many you bought. Hours you bought for a plan are valid until the plan's last day. A free hour is valid for 60 days from the day it is added (see The first hour free, Free hours and Recommend a family). The last day is shown in your view.
  • How the hours are used. The hours pay for your sessions automatically. When you propose a session, free hours are taken first, the plan's free hour and free hours we have added, and then the bought hours that expire first, and if the tutor suggests another time, they stay with the session. If the tutor declines, you withdraw the proposal or the day passes without an answer, the hours come back. If you buy hours, we add free hours or hours come back, while you have proposed or booked sessions coming up that are not paid, they pay for them in date order, as far as they go. Each started hour of the session uses one hour, except an hour that is free for a recommendation (see Recommend a family or a study helper): then the hours pay for the rest of the session. What is left of the last bought hour is saved in your time bank. If the hours in the purchase used first do not cover the whole session, the rest is taken from the next one, in the same order, and a session is taken from at most two purchases. If a session is shorter than booked, the hours come back in reverse order, so that the ones taken first are the ones used. The hours cover sessions with one child; a session with more children is paid by card, as is a session the hours do not cover.
  • The time bank. The time bank holds the hours you bought, with a plan, for a plan or earlier with Fill the time bank, free hours we have added, minutes that have been saved and hours you received for recommendations (see Recommend a family or a study helper). If a session paid with bought hours ends before a full hour, the rest is saved in your time bank, counted per started quarter of an hour: if a two-hour session lasted 1 hour and 45 minutes, 15 minutes are saved. If a later session runs over, the time is taken from the time bank first, at no cost, as far as the minutes go and when the session is for one child. If the minutes cover a whole session, they pay for the next session you propose, unless hours do. The time bank belongs to the family, siblings share it, and the saved minutes do not expire. How long the bought hours are valid is set out above.
  • The first hour free. The first plan you buy gets one extra hour, free: if you buy a plan with 8 hours you get 9. It is added to the plan once the plan is paid, and it is used first. It is valid for 60 days from the purchase, even if the plan's other hours have expired. If you stop early it counts like the other hours (see Stopping below), and it is never paid out as money. A family gets at most one such hour; hours for recommendations (see Recommend a family or a study helper) and free hours (below) do not count here.
  • Free hours. We may add hours to your time bank at no cost, for example when you start with us. They are valid for 60 days from the day we added them, and the last day is shown in your view. They pay for sessions like bought hours and are used first, with the other free hours. What is left of a free hour when a session ends before a full hour is not saved, and free hours that are not used expire. They are a gift: they do not count as bought hours and are never paid out as money.
  • Custom package. We can send your family a custom package, for example hours for a school holiday, and you then get an email about it. It is a plan with a different number of hours, at the plans' hourly price when we send it, and it is shown in your view under Payment & plans for as long as it can be bought. It is bought once: once it is bought it is no longer there, and if you want another one we send you a new one. It is bought and valid like a plan: for 30 days from the day it is paid, and what is set out here, under Stopping and under Right of withdrawal also applies to it. If it is the first plan you buy it comes with the first hour free, and if you have a family discount it is deducted from every hour.
  • Buy more hours. If you have a plan that is valid for at least one more day, you can buy 1–20 more hours at a time, under Your time bank in your view, also for a custom package. They cost the plan's hourly price, what you paid per hour for the plan, and expire on the same day as the plan. They are a separate purchase with the same rules as the plan: what is set out here, under Stopping and under Right of withdrawal also applies to them, and they come with no free hour.
  • Cancelling. You cancel a session paid with hours yourselves in your view, like an unpaid session. The hours then come back and stay valid until the same day as before. If you cancel less than 12 hours before the start, half the time is taken from the hours (see Cancel at least 12 hours before), and the rest comes back. If an hour is lost because we or the tutor cancelled too late for you to use it, we refund it at the price you paid.
  • Stopping. If you want to stop before the hours are used, we refund what is left. The hours you have already used are then counted at the regular hourly price, since a plan's discount applies because you buy all the hours. If you had a family discount when you bought the hours, the hours used are counted at the regular hourly price with that discount taken off. During the first 14 days after the purchase, the right of withdrawal applies instead, and the hours used are then counted at the price you paid. Minutes in the time bank are refunded at the regular hourly price, with the family discount taken off if your latest purchase of hours had one.
  • Hours that have not been used. Bought hours left when the period has ended expire. Ten days before, we email you if you have bought hours left. Get in touch before then if you will not have time to use them, and we refund the rest as described in the point above.

Price guarantee

What you paid for a plan, for hours for a plan or for Fill the time bank is the price for all the hours in the purchase. If we raise the hourly price in the meantime, the hours you have already bought do not cost more, and you never pay a difference. If you stop, the hours used are counted at the regular hourly price that applied when you bought them, not at a later, higher one.

Grade guarantee

If the student does their part, we do ours. If the conditions below are met and the grade that counts in a subject is not higher than the one the student had when you signed up, you get 10 hours of homework help free of charge, in total for the subjects concerned. The guarantee does not promise a grade, because the grade is set by the student’s teacher. It promises what we do if the grade does not go up.

  • Signing up. You sign up for the guarantee in your view, under Profile: you choose the subjects and enter the student’s current grade in each. It applies from the sign-up, to at most three subjects per student and school year. The sign-up is locked once it is made: you cannot change it, and a subject signed up for cannot be swapped for another. If something has gone wrong, we correct it when you write to us, and your view then shows when the sign-up was last changed.
  • By 31 December. The guarantee must be signed up for, and the student must have had their first session in the subject after signing up, by 31 December. Sign-up for a school year opens on 1 July. If you sign up later, or the sessions start later, the guarantee does not apply that school year.
  • The grade that counts is the year group’s last grade: the one the school sets in the subject when the school year ends. This applies to every year group, not only year 9. The Christmas grade does not count, and if the school sets no grade in the subject when the school year ends, the guarantee does not apply that school year.
  • A grade to compare with. The grade you enter when signing up must be the student’s latest grade in the subject from the school. If it is A, the subject cannot be signed up for. When you make a claim you show a copy of it, and the grade on the copy is the one that applies.
  • Two hours a week. From the first session after signing up until the end of the school year, the student has homework help for at least two hours every school week, in total across the subjects the guarantee covers, counted on the time actually held. Holiday weeks do not count. We see which subject a session covered in the report.
  • Time in each subject. If the grade does not go up in a subject because you have focused more on another of the subjects with the guarantee, the guarantee does not apply to that subject. We see how the time was split between the subjects in the reports.
  • Other subjects. We will of course help with other subjects and with homework in general whenever you need it, and that does not affect the guarantee. Those hours just do not count towards the two hours a week.
  • Missed hours. A week with fewer than two hours is made up within 14 days, whoever cancelled. If we or the tutor cancel, it is enough that you take the new time we suggest.
  • No missed sessions. If the student misses a session without having cancelled it, the guarantee no longer applies.
  • The tasks. At least 90 percent of the tasks the tutor has set in the subject during the period are done by their due date, as shown in your view. A task in NexLäx is done when the level is done.
  • Payment. All sessions during the period are paid.
  • Making a claim. Write to us within 30 days of the grade that counts being set, with a copy of it and of the latest grade before signing up, for each subject. We only check what is written here, from what is in your view: time held, the reports, missed sessions, the tasks and the payments. You see the same figures as we do, and we do not judge whether the student has been engaged. You get an answer within 14 days. We keep the copies of the grades until the claim is decided, and then we delete them.
  • The hours. You get 10 hours in total, even if the grade did not go up in several subjects. They apply to the same student and the subjects where the grade did not go up, and must be used within six months of our answer. They are booked as ordinary sessions, and you pay nothing for them. They are not money: they cannot be exchanged, are not refunded if you stop and cannot be given to anyone else.
  • The guarantee can be used once per student and subject. We may change the grade guarantee or stop offering it, but a guarantee you have already signed up for applies until the grade that counts.

Recommend a family or a study helper

Every family and study helper with an account has their own code, under Profile in their view. A family's code works in the enquiry form, where a new family signs up. A study helper's code works in the job application, where someone applies to become a study helper. Whoever signs up or applies with the code, through the link or by typing it in the form, got the recommendation from you. A code typed in the wrong form does not count, and the enquiry or application goes through anyway.

  • An hour in the time bank. When a new family that signed up with your code has had two hours of homework help with us, in sessions that took place, and we have approved the recommendation, you get one hour of homework help for free in your time bank. This also applies if the family got one of the hours free. New means that the family had not had any session with us before it signed up. You get one hour per family you recommended, and if a family got several codes, the first one it signed up with counts.
  • How the hour is used. It is deducted from the next homework help session you propose, one hour per session, at that session's own hourly price. You pay for the rest of a longer session as usual: with hours in your time bank if you have any, otherwise by card. The hour is valid for 60 days from the day the new family had its two hours, or from the day we approved the recommendation if that came later, and it goes to a session you propose within that time, for a day within it. If the session is cancelled, or the study helper says no to the time, the hour comes back and stays valid until the same day as before.
  • The hour is not money. It cannot be exchanged, and it is not paid back if you stop.
  • If you are a study helper: the referral bonus. When someone who applied for a job with your code has got their study helper account with us, you get 300 kronor in pay, including holiday pay and before tax, once we have approved the bonus. It is paid with the pay for the month we approved it, on the 25th of the month after. It applies to a person who is new to us: not yourself, not someone who has applied for a job with us before and not someone who already had an account. It is given once per person, and you need to be an approved study helper when the account is created. If we turn the application down, there is no bonus.
  • We approve the hour and the bonus. The hour and the bonus are registered automatically, but you only get them once we have reviewed and approved them. We only check what is written here: that the person who signed up or applied is new, that the code is yours and that the terms are met. If we refuse an hour or a bonus we give a reason, and you can ask us about it at info@nextrum.se.
  • What you see. You see how many signed up or applied with your code and how many of them became customers or got an account, but not who. A study helper also sees the status of each bonus, and the first name of the person who applied with the code once that person has an account.
  • We may switch off a code that is used in a way it is not meant for, for example in advertising that looks like it comes from us. The offers may change or end; what you have already earned remains.

Right of withdrawal

If you book a session here on the site, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal under the Swedish Distance and Off-Premises Contracts Act. The withdrawal period starts on the day the session is booked, that is, when both you and the tutor have said yes to the time.

  • You withdraw by telling us within 14 days, at info@nextrum.se. You do not need to give a reason. You can use the form below, but you do not have to: an email that clearly says you are withdrawing from the booking is enough.
  • If you have paid for the session, we refund the full amount no later than 14 days after we receive your notice, to the card you paid with or to Klarna. If you have not paid yet, you pay nothing for a session that has not started.
  • Most sessions take place within the withdrawal period. When you suggest a time, you are therefore asking us to hold the session even though the period has not yet ended. If you withdraw after the session has started, you pay for the part that has taken place. A session that has been fully held cannot be withdrawn from.
  • If you withdraw from a booking within the withdrawal period before the session has started, we do not charge the fee for a late cancellation: you then pay nothing for the session, as above.
  • If you buy a plan or hours for a plan, the same right of withdrawal applies, counted from the day of purchase. If you withdraw, we refund what you paid, except for the hours already used. Those are counted at the hourly price you paid for the plan or the hours, not at the regular price.

If you are a tutor

  • You apply, we interview, and you can start only once you have been approved.
  • You decide your own hours, your subjects and how much you work.
  • We agree your hourly rate together, and you can see your hours and statements in your own view. Payment is made for sessions that were completed and reported, and as a bonus for recommendations (see Recommend a family or a study helper).
  • All payment goes through Nextrum. Never accept payment directly from a family — that is the whole basis for the parent having oversight.
  • You must not move the relationship into your own business on the side. This is not a formality: the family chose us because we vetted you, and outside the platform none of those guarantees apply.

How to behave

Contact takes place in the platform’s chat, so that the parent can see what is said. Abusive, threatening or inappropriate behaviour leads to the account being closed. That applies in both directions.

Our liability

We are responsible for arranging and running the service. We are not responsible for study results beyond what the grade guarantee promises, nor for matters outside our control — such as outages at our suppliers.

If something goes wrong because of us, we compensate at most what you paid over the previous three months. That limit does not apply if we caused the damage intentionally or through gross negligence.

If you are a consumer, the mandatory rules of consumer law always take precedence over what is written here. Nothing in these terms limits them.

Ending the agreement

You can stop whenever you like. Paid sessions that have not taken place are refunded, except the fee for a late cancellation, and what is left in the time bank, bought hours and saved minutes, is refunded as described under Plans and the time bank. Free hours are not refunded. We may close an account that breaches these terms, and will say why.

Changes

We may change these terms. We give at least 30 days’ notice of material changes to those who have an account. If you do not accept them you can stop before they take effect.

Disputes

Swedish law applies. If we cannot resolve a dispute between us, it can be examined by the Swedish National Board for Consumer Disputes (ARN) or by a Swedish general court.
