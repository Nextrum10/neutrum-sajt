How applying works
01Apply

Fill in the application with subjects, year groups and times that suit you. Takes a few minutes.

02Interview

A short conversation where we get to know you and how you explain things.

03Get approved

We run our checks and you get your tutor profile.

04Start working

Accept requests and hold your first session.

Who can apply

You do not need to be a teacher. You need to be able to explain.

  • 01
    You are at upper-secondary school or university

    Both upper-secondary and university students are welcome to apply.

  • 02
    You are confident in your subjects

    You do not have to be top of the class in everything — just sure of what you want to help with.

  • 03
    You can explain so that someone understands

    That is the most important quality, and the one we look at most in the interview.

  • 04
    You keep your word

    The student does best with the same person week after week. If you say yes to a session, you turn up.

If you are under 18 we may need a guardian’s consent before your first session. We will get in touch if that applies to you.
