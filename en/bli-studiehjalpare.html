How applying works
1Apply

Fill in the application with subjects, year groups and times that suit you. Takes a few minutes.

2Interview

A short conversation where we get to know you and how you explain things.

3Get approved

We run our checks and you get your tutor profile.

4Start working

Accept requests and hold your first session.

Who can apply

You do not need to be a teacher.
You need to be able to explain.

  • You are in upper secondary school or olderYou need to have started upper secondary school. After that you do not need to be studying: anyone allowed to work is welcome to apply.
  • You are confident in your subjectsYou do not have to be top of the class in everything — just sure of what you want to help with.
  • You can explain so that someone understandsThat is the most important quality, and the one we look at most in the interview.
  • You keep your wordThe student does best with the same person week after week. If you say yes to a session, you turn up.

If you are under 18 we need written approval from your parent or guardian before you can start. You add their email in the application, and we email them.
