Does it cost anything to send?

No. Getting in touch is free and commits you to nothing. You only pay once you have booked a session.

How quickly do you reply?

We normally reply within a couple of days. If you are not sure exactly what you need help with, an approximate answer is fine — we work it out together.

What happens to the details?

They are used only to contact you about your enquiry. Nothing is shared further. How we handle your data.
