NexLäx

Practice that
feels like a game.

NexLäx is Nextrum’s own digital practice between sessions: short levels in school subjects, where every answer is marked straight away. Students collect XP and badges, the tutor sees what has stuck, and you see the same in your view.

  1. A path through the subject

    Topics in order, level by level, one step at a time. The next step opens when the one before is done, and students choose any subject and year.

  2. Marked on the spot

    Choose, type, match or put in order. A wrong answer shows the right one and why, and the question comes back.

  3. Stars, XP and badges

    Days in a row, today’s challenges and badges to collect. What shows is what the student has actually done.

  4. Tied to the sessions

    The tutor sets levels that go with the session and sees how they went before the next one.

In practice

What a session can look like.

Sessions differ by subject and need, but the rhythm usually looks like this.

  • Before

    You choose the time

    Book within the tutor’s hours, online or in person, one to three hours.

  • During

    The student works with their tutor

    The tutor works from the study plan and from what was hard last time. The goal is not to finish the homework but to understand it.

  • After

    The report is written

    What you went through, how it went and what the next step is. You read it in the student view the same evening.

What you get

Four things we stand behind.

None of this is an add-on. It is included in the hourly price, whatever the subject.

  • 1

    Personal matching

    We choose the tutor based on subject, level, need and personality — not on who happens to be free. If the match feels wrong, we redo it.

  • 2

    An individual study plan

    Written for the student, not for the year group. It starts from where the student actually is and is updated as you go.

  • 3

    A report after every session

    So progress can be followed between sessions. The report is also what makes a session count as completed.

  • 4

    Safety at every step

    Interviewed and trained tutors, with payment and communication gathered in the platform.
