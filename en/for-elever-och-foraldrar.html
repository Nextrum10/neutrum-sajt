The hardest part of tutoring is rarely the session itself. It is knowing what happens between them.

The student view

Everything in one place.

Once the match is made, the student view unlocks. It holds the study plan, upcoming sessions, messages and the report from each session.

The report is written by the tutor after the session and describes what you went through and how it went. It is not a formality — a session counts as completed only once it has been written.

Student and parent see the same thing. Nobody has to ask how it went, and nobody has to remember to tell.

In practice

What a session can look like.

Sessions differ by subject and need, but the rhythm usually looks like this.

Before You choose the time

Book within the tutor’s hours, online or in person, one to three hours.

During The student works with their tutor

The tutor works from the study plan and from what was hard last time. The goal is not to finish the homework but to understand it.

After The report is written

What you went through, how it went and what the next step is. You read it in the student view the same evening.

What you get

Four things we stand behind.

None of this is an add-on. It is included in the hourly price, whatever the subject.

We choose the tutor based on subject, level, need and personality — not on who happens to be free. If the match feels wrong, we redo it.

Written for the student, not for the year group. It starts from where the student actually is and is updated as you go.

So progress can be followed between sessions. The report is also what makes a session count as completed.

Verified tutors, with payment and communication gathered in the platform.
